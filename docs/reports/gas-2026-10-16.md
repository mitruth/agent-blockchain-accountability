# Gas per order, per policy and combined (step 4)

Measured on 16 October 2026 by `test/policies/Step4Acceptance.t.sol`
(`test_gas_*`), under Hardhat 3.15, Solidity 0.8.25, optimizer 200 runs,
EVM cancun.

**What is measured.**

- **Scope:** the gas of the agent's whole submission, through its session on
  `MiniAccount`, the gate, the account and the ported market's prosumer
  contract and session.
- **Orders:** bids of 100 Wh at 0.25 per kWh.
- **Steady state:** each figure is taken after a first submission of the same
  kind, so cold storage of the first order is not counted.
- **Windows:** sliding, 24 one-hour buckets for S1 and S2, 12 five-minute
  buckets for S6, with caps that never bind.

| Policies installed | One bid | Overhead | 24-leg batch | Overhead | Cancel | Overhead |
| ------------------ | ------: | -------: | -----------: | -------: | -----: | -------: |
| None | 304,906 | | 6,509,320 | | 73,681 | |
| `StaticOrderPolicy` (A1 to A4, A6) | 320,477 | +15,571 | 6,666,915 | +157,595 | 86,213 | +12,532 |
| S1, owner budget | 326,675 | +21,769 | 6,591,119 | +81,799 | 99,360 | +25,679 |

| All five instances | 404,978 | **+100,072** | 6,975,299 | **+465,979** | 201,909 | **+128,228** |

**Reading it.**

- **One order.** All five instances add about 100,000 gas, a third of a bare
  order. The overhead is close to the sum of the parts.
- **A day-ahead batch.** All five add 466,000 gas over 24 legs, 7% of the
  batch, about 19,400 a leg. A window policy charges a batch once, so S1, S2 and
  S6 grow little with the leg count. `StaticOrderPolicy` checks every leg,
  including a decoder call for the slot's start.
- **A cancellation.** It costs more with policies than an order costs. Each
  instance is credited back, and S6 counts the cancellation.
- **Not included:** the worst-case window sweep. A write after 167 idle hourly
  buckets of a week's window costs about 340,000 gas
  (`test_theWorstCaseSweepOfAWeekOfHourlyBucketsIsBounded`). The 24-bucket
  windows here sweep at most 23.

The same 24-leg batch with all five instances is 7.0 million gas, well within a
30 million block. The real-account overhead of a Smart Session user operation
is not part of these figures.
